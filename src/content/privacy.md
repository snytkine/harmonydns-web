# Privacy policy

_Last updated: November 25, 2026_

Harmony DNS is built to keep your DNS private. This policy explains what the Harmony DNS app and this website do with your information.

## The Harmony DNS app

### Data stays on your Mac

Harmony DNS stores your zones, records, settings and an 8-hour log of DNS queries on your Mac only. The query log can be cleared in the app at any time, and older entries are deleted automatically.

### No data collection

The app has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising, and it sends nothing to the developer. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

### DNS queries

DNS queries that are not answered from your own zones or the cache are sent to the upstream DNS server you choose (standard DNS, DNS over HTTPS or DNS over TLS). That provider, such as Cloudflare, Google, NextDNS or AdGuard, receives those queries and has its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to read.

### VPN configuration

Harmony DNS uses a macOS VPN configuration only to answer the DNS questions your Mac asks. No other network traffic passes through it, and no traffic is sent to the developer.

## This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. Your light or dark mode choice is saved in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to operate the service; see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children

Harmony DNS does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
