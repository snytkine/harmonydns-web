# Your Mac's DNS, in harmony.

Harmony DNS is a macOS menu bar app that answers every DNS query on your Mac. It serves the DNS zones you manage yourself and sends everything else, encrypted, to the upstream server you trust.

- **Administer your own DNS zones.** Perfect for home labs and small businesses: give real DNS names to the IP addresses on your local network.
- **Secure DNS over HTTPS** for enhanced privacy and security of every DNS request on your Mac, not just the ones made by your browser.
- **Block ads and tracking** in all browsers and all apps by choosing AdGuard DNS as your upstream server.
- **Detailed logs** of all DNS requests made by every browser and app on your Mac.
- **Faster browsing** with enhanced DNS caching that keeps names resolving even when an upstream server hiccups.
